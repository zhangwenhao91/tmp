1. 看残留构建的内容和时间(应该明显比顶层 build 旧):

ls -la --time-style=full-iso /home/zwh/tilelang-tileir-ascend/3rdparty/tvm/build/lib/
ls -la --time-style=full-iso /home/zwh/tilelang-tileir-ascend/build/lib/

2. 确认是谁在引用它(看 NEEDED / RPATH):

cd /home/zwh/tilelang-tileir-ascend
for f in build/lib/*.so; do
  echo "=== $f ==="
  readelf -d "$f" 2>/dev/null | grep -E "NEEDED.*libtvm|RPATH|RUNPATH"
done

3. 顺带看一眼 site-packages 里 editable 的 tilelang/lib 装的是什么:

ls -la /usr/local/python3.11.0/lib/python3.11/site-packages/tilelang/lib/
